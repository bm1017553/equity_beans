Coffee that pays its growers fairly

Equity means fairness. Every coffee we sell is single-origin and Fair Trade certified, bought from the cooperatives of small farmers who grow it.

What Fair Trade means

A fair minimum price

Cooperatives are paid at least a set minimum for their coffee, which protects farmers when market prices drop.

A premium for communities

On top of that price, cooperatives earn a Fair Trade premium and vote on how to spend it, on things like schools, clean water, or better farm equipment.

Standards for people and land

Certified farms follow rules for fair working conditions, no forced or child labor, and protecting the land they farm.

Why it costs a bit more

Our coffee costs more than most supermarket coffee. That difference is the fair price and premium going to the people who grow it, and beans you can trace back to one country instead of a blend from everywhere.
