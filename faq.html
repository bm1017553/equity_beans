Ordering & shipping

How much is shipping?

Shipping is per order, and free on orders of or more. Your cart shows how close you are to free shipping.

Is there a limit on how many bags I can order?

You can order up to of each coffee and purchase option per order. If you need more, send us a message.

Do I need an account to order?

No, you can check out as a guest. If you log in or create an account during the same visit, we’ll save that order to your account so you can find it again.

How do I pay?

Card payments through Square are coming soon. For now the site is in demo mode: you can place an order and get an order number, but no payment is taken.

Our coffee

What’s the difference between the purchase options?

Ground & Roasted is ready to brew. Whole Bean & Roasted stays fresh longer if you grind it yourself. Whole Bean & Unroasted is green coffee for people who roast at home, and it costs less.

How big is a bag?

Every price on the site is for a 1 lb bag.

What do light, medium and dark roast mean?

Light roasts are brighter and more fruity or floral. Dark roasts are bolder and smoother, with less acidity. Medium sits in between. Every coffee shows its roast as a tag, and the shop can filter by it.

Why does a coffee say “sold out” or “out of stock”?

“Sold out” next to a purchase option means that version is gone, but the others can still be ordered. “Out of stock” means the whole coffee is unavailable for now.

Is all of your coffee Fair Trade?

Yes, every coffee we sell is Fair Trade certified and single-origin. Read what Fair Trade means for the farmers.

Accounts & the coffee quiz

What do I get with an account?

You can review coffees, keep your coffee quiz matches, and see every order you’ve placed on your account page.

How does the coffee quiz pick my match?

It asks six quick questions. The flavors you pick count the most, then the region and time of day you choose, and how you drink your coffee. Flavors you’d rather avoid push a coffee down the list. Your brewing method picks the right grind. Take the quiz.
